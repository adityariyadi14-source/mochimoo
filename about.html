Our story since 2021

A little kitchen, a lot of heart.

mochimoo started as a small home-kitchen project in Bandung. We make familiar snacks feel special through thoughtful preparation, honest service, and packaging that is ready to gift or share.

What matters to us

Good food, honest care.

We believe good snacks start with care. We pay attention to taste, texture, and how every order reaches you.

Our menu brings together chewy Dubai cookies, crispy cheesticks, and golden risoles. Each one has its own character, so you can choose according to your mood.

We call our customers “mooers”. Thank you for supporting a small local business and being part of the mochimoo story.
